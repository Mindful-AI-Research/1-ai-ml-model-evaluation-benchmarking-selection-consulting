The right scheme depends on the data structure

The practical rule is simple: choose the method that protects the dependency structure in your data and avoids artificial optimism.

SituationSchemeWhy
ClassificationStratifiedKFoldPreserves class proportions in each fold.
Regression / independent dataKFoldSimple rotation is usually appropriate.
Repeated entity observationsGroupKFoldPrevents the same entity from appearing on both sides.
Time-ordered dataTimeSeriesSplitTrains on the past and tests on the future, without temporal leakage.
Very limited dataLOO / RepeatedStratifiedKFoldUses examples more intensively, with computational trade-offs.

Stratification reduces instability in classification

What it does

More balanced distribution of positive cases

Distributes positive cases more evenly across folds. This stabilizes evaluation, but it does not create missing minority examples.

When it helps most

Cases in which to prioritize it

  • Imbalanced classes.
  • Few positives per fold.
  • Metrics sensitive to prevalence.

GroupKFold protects against entity leakage

It keeps each entity entirely on one side. It is the right defense when different rows belong to the same person, patient, customer, or device.

Wrong

The same patient on both sides

The same patient in training and validation.

Right

Indivisible entity

The whole patient stays on one side only.

Effect

A more realistic estimate

A less optimistic estimate that is more faithful to real use.

Repetition measures uncertainty better

RepeatedStratifiedKFold repeats stratified validation with multiple random partitions, making the estimate more informative about uncertainty.
